# Cultures

Institutions, customs, trade, sport and society — the texture of the island, which
the bible treats as mattering as much as the battles.

Recorded, not yet written up:

the High Church · the older folk faith · the druid trio (druid, novice, keeper) ·
ring-money (1 ring; 5 rings = 1 amulet; 13 amulets = 1 crown = 65 rings) ·
blackweed · the Saltwater Cup · Araby and its trade ·
Fimir brood society: age and precedence, birthdays, presents, feuds
