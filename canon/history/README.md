# History

Events and sequences.

The established spine: the Old Ones raise the pylons and depart · the Ancients remain
as custodians · the Fimir arrive · humans arrive · Argant drives the Fimir toward the
salt coasts.

Argant is not the Sleeping King. Keep them in separate entries so they cannot drift
together.

Dates, reign lengths and named battles are unknown. An entry may say so; it may not
supply them.
