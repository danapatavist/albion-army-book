# Magic

How magic works on Albion, and the system underneath it.

The pylons are the centre of this folder: Old Ones' infrastructure, still running,
maintained by people who understand it as ritual rather than engineering.

Recorded, not yet written up:

the pylon network · pylon sites absorbed into barrows, witch huts, caves, folds,
standing stones, hill forts and masonry · misaligned pylons as Beastman ingress ·
druidic craft: fire, rain, stone-moving · Root and Knots · ember druids ·
brood-mother magic and the small hearth
