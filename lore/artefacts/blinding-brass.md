# Blinding Brass

The **Blinding Brass** is made from **many pieces of brass, hammered together and highly polished** to direct light at giants, **blinding them and driving them away**. Its purpose is deterrence rather than necessarily killing them.

In the everyday life of Field and Fenn communities, **war machines are primarily anti-giant machines**: practical defences against dangerous giants, rather than inventions made principally for human warfare.

## Unresolved
The precise shape, arrangement of the brass pieces, light source, and tabletop rules have not yet been established.
