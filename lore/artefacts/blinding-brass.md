# Blinding Brass

The **Blinding Brass** directs light to **blind giants and drive them away**. Its purpose is deterrence rather than necessarily killing them.

In the everyday life of Field and Fenn communities, **war machines are primarily anti-giant machines**: practical defences against dangerous giants, rather than inventions made principally for human warfare.

## Unresolved
The mechanism by which the light is directed, its source, and exact tabletop rules have not yet been established.
