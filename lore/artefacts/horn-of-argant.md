# Horn Shrine of Argant

## Established lore
**Alfran Longbritches** has recovered the enormous ancient **Horn of Argant**, rather than a summoning gem. A Blaewen hero sounds it to summon the **Waiting Guard**, ancient drowned warriors preserved in Albion's bogs. The Guard await Argant, not Alfran.

The horn is carried as a **portable shrine or reliquary**: a monumental instrument mounted on a sturdy wooden frame. A dedicated, strong **Horn Bearer** steadies its weight while a **Blaewen hero blows it**. This creates a distinct two-person arrangement, with the bearer acting as a living stand.

The great horn has a **genuinely open, hollow bell** through which sound emerges; the end must not be sealed by sculpture or a decorative face. The shrine may have bronze mounts, worn cloth and votive ornaments.

## Appearance and setting
The approved working visual direction is an ancient Albion relic borne among Blaewen troops while the Waiting Guard rise from wet bogland. Albion's setting is **Anglo-Celtic and prehistoric/early historic**, with barrows, pylons, standing stones, marshes and timber constructions. **Do not introduce Arthurian or medieval stone castles into background illustrations.**

## Developing game concept
The horn might require an accompanying hero to sound it and a bearer to support it before the Waiting Guard can join the battle. Precise deployment, roll mechanics, models and unit restrictions remain to be determined.

## Nature of the summoned host
The Waiting Guard are a kind of **waterlogged, bog-preserved zombie**. Their ancient association with Argant does not mean they can now recognise a king or choose whom to obey: the horn summons them and they fight. Their ancient history is tragic, but they are not conscious or articulate in battle.
