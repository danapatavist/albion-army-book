# Stinger Eel Flog

A **Field and Fenn catapult** which hurls a **net sack containing an electric eel or stinging eel**. Field and Fenn war machines are principally used against giants in everyday life.

## Unresolved
Whether the animal is an electric eel or a distinct kind of stinging eel, the precise effect of the projectile, and game rules have not been established.
