# Stinger Eel Flog

A **Field and Fenn catapult** which hurls a **net sack containing an electric eel or stinging eel**. Field and Fenn war machines are principally used against giants in everyday life.

## Design shown in concept art
The mobile catapult has a **wheeled wooden frame and a long wicker dragon-shaped throwing arm**, with fins, eyes, and an open-jawed head. A crew loads a live eel into a **weighted net with hooks** inside the wicker throwing cup, pulls the arm down, aims, and releases it. The crew are depicted operating the ropes and mechanism alongside the machine.

## Unresolved
Whether the animal is an electric eel or a distinct kind of stinging eel, the precise effect of the projectile, and game rules have not been established.
