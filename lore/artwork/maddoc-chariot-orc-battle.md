# Maddoc's Chariot — Orc Battle Illustration

**Approved full-page codex artwork** depicting Maddoc the Flame charging into a mass of Orcs in her fast, lightweight Red Host war chariot.

## Composition
- Dynamic low-angle, head-on impact with a wall of Orc fighters, broken shields, dust, and flying debris.
- **Two trained male lions with great manes** pull the chariot, smashing into the Orc formation. Their obedience is training, not magical Binding.
- **Maddoc** stands at the front of the chariot, red-haired, bearing a spear and fighting fiercely.
- Her personal **Oiler / war-painter / attendant** rides alongside her as a fighting bodyguard, with a **large round bronze shield** and short sword.
- Chariot is a **light, fast vehicle**, not a cumbersome ceremonial wagon.
- Decoration should feature **totems rather than flags**, as established in the revised chariot model.
- Rich classic Warhammer Fantasy army-book / codex action illustration, portrait full-page format; strong reds, bronzes, and earth tones.

## Background wall

A tall fortified stone wall appears behind the Orcs in this illustration. **Its identity is not established in canon.** It may be read simply as an Orc-held stronghold or a fortress under assault; avoid naming the location without further agreement.
