# The Beast of the Blaewen Court

## Established lore
The **Beast** is a physically imposing member of Alfran Longbritches' court and a natural guardian of **Elfred Sidesaddle**, whom he understands and protects without trying to turn him into Alfran. His background includes service in the court of an **Araby prince**.

**The Beast has a white tiger** associated with him at the Blaewen court. Both belong to the roster of striking characters and beasts associated with the Ridings.

## Developing game concept
The Beast and his white tiger may be a **paired tabletop character or special choice**. Whether they always deploy together, use separate bases, or have particular fighting roles is not yet decided.

## Unresolved
The tiger's name, origin, exact relationship with the Beast and battlefield rules have not been established.
