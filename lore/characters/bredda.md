# Bredda
Bredda is one of **three sisters**: Maddoc the Flame, Bredda and Helda. She is a large, strong, butch warrior and commander of the Red Host's **Bronze Guard**, an elite fighting regiment whose distinctive armour is fashioned to resemble overlapping **pub brasses**: flat, decorated brass plaques with rustic imagery. Bredda is intended to be a playable named character, but no rules are assigned yet.
