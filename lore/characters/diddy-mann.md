# Diddy Mann

## Established canon
- **Diddy Mann is a tiny lizard wizard**, associated with the Field and Fenn.
- His **woodwose is his mount**, but rather than sitting astride it, Diddy Mann rides **on its shoulder**.
- **Diddy and his woodwose always enter battle together** as a single character choice; Diddy is not fielded independently.
- Diddy has **negligible physical strength and no meaningful attack**.
- **Diddy continues casting spells from the woodwose's shoulder while the woodwose fights.**
- Diddy **cannot truly die**: if he is 'killed' in battle, he simply **leaves the battlefield**. The exact rules representation remains to be decided.

## Unresolved
The nature of his magic, exact size, the woodwose's name and further details of their partnership remain to be detailed.
