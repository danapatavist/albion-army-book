# Helda
Helda is one of **three sisters**: Maddoc the Flame, Bredda and Helda. She loves animals and is a Red Host **Binder** (sometimes called a lion warg). She has **three lionesses**, and magically binds into her **favourite lioness** while the other two follow that lioness naturally. This is **one magical bond**, not three simultaneous possessions. Helda's human body **remains behind the fighting** rather than running alongside the pride. Her creature association is reflected in the Red Host **Lion Wargers** concept art. No rules yet.
