# The Knotwife

## Established lore
The Knotwife is a witch associated with the Field and Fenn. She carries a cane ornamented with impossible knots and has a frog familiar.

The knots on the cane **represent the nature of her magic**; the cane is not necessarily its source. Her witchcraft can **tie people in knots** and **tongue-tie a rival**, interfering with the rival's ability to speak.

## Unresolved
Whether the effects are literal, figurative or both in different circumstances; their duration and limits; any tabletop rules. The origins of her cane and frog are not settled.
