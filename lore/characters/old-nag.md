# Old Nag

## Established lore
**Old Nag** is one of **the Ancients**, the last surviving humanoid custodians left by the Old Ones to maintain Albion's pylons.

She belongs to the **Salt / Grey** sphere, not the Field and Fenn. She is a **ten-foot-tall Ancient and sea witch**. Her witchcraft uses **eggs**: she takes eggs to the sea and through them raises **waves and fog**. The precise ritual meaning of giving or throwing eggs into the sea is not yet defined. Being a witch does not place her politically among the Green communities.

Old Nag is supported by an unusual druidic trio: a **Druid**, a **Robinson Novice** and a **Sword**. Robinsons rarely become druidic novices, and novices are conventionally gelded.

## Acolytes and attendants
Earlier development describes predominantly older, bearded male acolytes who catalogue shells, eggs, tides, weather, omens and specimens, and help interpret her workings. A young Grey-coast Robinson/Ribbin trainee is especially unusual. The precise relationship between these acolytes and the Druid–Novice–Sword trio remains to be settled.

## Unresolved
Her particular pylon duties, the exact egg rituals, the identities of her attendants and how older names **Old Neg** and **Old Gul / White Gull** relate to Old Nag. Do not merge those identities without confirmation.
