# Old Nag

## Established lore
**Old Nag** is one of **the Ancients**, the last surviving humanoid custodians left by the Old Ones to maintain Albion's pylons.

She belongs to the **Salt / Grey** sphere, not the Field and Fenn. Although she can be described as a hag or witch, that does not place her politically among the Green communities.

Old Nag is supported by an unusual druidic trio: a **Druid**, a **Robinson Novice** and a **Sword**. Robinsons rarely become druidic novices, and novices are conventionally gelded.

## Unresolved
Her precise magic, coastal role, pylon duties, visual design and the identities of her attendants.
