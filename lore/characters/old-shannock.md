# Old Shannock

## Established lore
- An ancient blind wood wizard, with pale white eyes and long nails, associated with Albion's old powers and the Field and Fenn.
- Sits upon a moving throne of suspended stones, linked by vines and roots.
- Is accompanied by a small retinue of **druid warriors** or warrior-monks who guard him and his throne.

## Creator-supplied visual reference
The supplied concept sheet depicts Old Shannock enthroned amid a cluster of suspended carved megaliths and guarded by robed and armed druid warriors on floating stone platforms. Five presented miniature concepts are labelled **The Balance**, **The Keeper**, **The Listener**, **The Warden**, and **The Judgement**. Treat these labels as *concept-sheet names pending confirmation*, not automatically established ranks or canonical individuals.

The warriors are human-looking, bearded, dressed in weathered cloaks and ritual garments, and carry staff-like weapons, spears or axes. The impression is of a small order of dedicated guardians rather than ordinary infantry.
