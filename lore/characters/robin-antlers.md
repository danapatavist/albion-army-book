# Robin Antlers

## Established lore
**Robin Antlers** is **Elfred Sidesaddle's husband**, associated with Elfred's **Court of Antlers** in the Blaewen Ridings. He is a classical heroic prince: handsome, gallant, highborn and beautifully mannered.

He is **not** Robin Fair, the fox-masked folk-king and outlaw of Field and Fenn.

## Unresolved
His personal military command, precise titles, weapons, relationship with Alfran and further background have not been established.
