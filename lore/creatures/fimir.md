# Fimir

## Established lore
The Fimir were driven toward the salt coasts in the founding tradition of Argant. Their life is organised around **broods and brood mothers**. A brood mother's magical ability deepens with age, beginning with the almost supernatural mastery of maintaining a small hearth.

Their disputes can escalate into warfare; rival broods only rarely seek a queen's judgement. Age matters deeply to social standing. Birthdays and gifts are important social practices.

Failed broods can give rise to **Skallies**, bleak, hungry, ghoul-like outcasts with flint knives and stones. They eat eggs and can sometimes be drawn together by primal music. They may steal a human baby and raise it among themselves.

Fimir material culture makes distinctive use of sharp flint. One relic is a jewel-encrusted comet-like object with a lidless eye, continually watered with brackish liquid by a witch.
