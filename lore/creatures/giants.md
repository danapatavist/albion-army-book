# Giants of Albion

**Giants are found across all of Albion**, not specifically within the Field and Fenn. They are generally regarded as **nuisances** rather than predatory monsters.

**As giants grow large, they lose their wits.** Giants tear down constructions that interfere with ancient **ley lines**, continuing an old role in maintaining the island's magical alignments. This can bring them into conflict with people living and building nearby.

Among the everyday uses of Albion's defensive war machines is **driving giants away**. The Field and Fenn's Blinding Brass is one example; it redirects bright light to dazzle giants.

## Unresolved
How much giants understand at different sizes or ages, and how their distribution and relationships differ between regions, remain to be established.
