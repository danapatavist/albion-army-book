# Giants of Albion

**Giants are found across all of Albion**, not specifically within the Field and Fenn. They are generally regarded as **nuisances** rather than predatory monsters.

**Giants lose their wits gradually as they grow.** The original intelligent **half-giant** form is roughly 8–10 feet tall: social, helpful, generous and musical. A period known as the **Great Growth**, lasting roughly 8–12 years, brings intense hunger, aching bones and relentless growth. As they become full giants—often 15–25 feet or more—their memory and reasoning deteriorate into a dementia-like state. Truly enormous giants are anomalous overgrowths rather than the original caretaker form. Giants tear down constructions that interfere with ancient **ley lines**, continuing an old role in maintaining the island's magical alignments. This can bring them into conflict with people living and building nearby.

Among the everyday uses of Albion's defensive war machines is **driving giants away**. The Field and Fenn's Blinding Brass is one example; it redirects bright light to dazzle giants.

## Unresolved
Detailed regional differences and the fates of individual giant lineages remain open. Named giants include Eddard the Dunn, Dunce Peter and Tam Loggins, who show different sizes and capacities.
