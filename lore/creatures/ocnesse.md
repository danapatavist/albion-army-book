# Oc'nesse

## Established lore
Oc'nesses are a kind of monstrous, Warhammerian **plesiosaur** associated with the **Blaewen Ridings**. They are a species rather than a unique legendary individual. Blaewen lords sometimes ride them; ram chariots are another noble riding or war conveyance.

**Shaggy** is an unusually old **bull Oc'nesse** ridden by **Alfran Longbritches**. He carries Alfran's raised wooden throne. Shaggy is especially large and distinguished by his throne and royal trappings.

## Developing miniature and army-book concept
A **generic hero or lord riding an Oc'nesse** may be fielded separately from the **named Alfran riding Shaggy** version. They share the same broad creature design, but the latter has its distinctive throne and regalia. No rules or points have been established.

## Unresolved
Their land-and-water capabilities, husbandry, precise prevalence, other named specimens, and rules are undecided. Their exact biological adaptations have not been established.
