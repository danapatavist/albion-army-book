# Woodwoses

## Established connections
- Woodwoses belong to Albion's woodland and Field and Fenn imagery.
- **Diddy Mann**, a tiny lizard wizard, rides on the **shoulder of his woodwose mount**, not astride its back.

## Visual references supplied by creator
The supplied miniature concepts depict woodwoses as **large, shaggy, human-faced forest giants**: broad and powerful, with long hair and beards, bare feet, moss/leaf-like shaggy covering and rough wooden staves or clubs. Their appearance is more thoughtful and human than bestial. One illustration depicts Diddy Mann riding high on the shoulder of a club-bearing woodwose. Other images show variations with carved stones and woodland ornaments.

These are visual references, **not automatic confirmation** of every inscription, measurement, symbol or piece of background text shown on the concept sheets.

## Unresolved
Exact height, intelligence, language, relationship to the Ancients and Old Shannock, broader society and rules are not yet confirmed.
