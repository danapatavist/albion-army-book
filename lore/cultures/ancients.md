# The Ancients

## Established lore
The **Ancients** are powerful old guardians of Albion. Their work is associated with maintaining the ancient structures that protect the island from Chaos. Some sleep, some persist in strange forms, and some are diminished or erratic; age does not guarantee wisdom.

The road-junction **Trickster** is understood as an Ancient whose increasingly unpredictable magic becomes tangled with impossible knots, five-way junctions, gift days and—through Alfran's reforming programme—roundabouts.

**Rhiannon** is believed to be an Ancient and is associated with fire magic.

## Unresolved
Their number, individual origins and relationship to the Old Ones are not established.
