# The Ancients

## Established lore
The **Ancients** are essentially the **last surviving humanoid beings left behind by the Old Ones to maintain Albion's pylons**. These ancient guardians are individuals, not the ordinary warrior-monks who accompany them. Pylons are part of the ancient works that hold back Chaos.

Known Ancients include **Old Shannock, Snakespeaker, Jenny (Genny) Willows, Old Nag and Father Winter**. The road-junction **Trickster** is also understood as an Ancient; his increasingly unpredictable magic becomes tangled with impossible knots, five-way junctions, gift days and, later, roundabouts. **Rhiannon** is believed to be an Ancient, with fire-associated powers.

Some Ancients sleep, some remain active in strange forms, and some have become erratic or diminished. Their great age does not guarantee clear judgement.

## Unresolved
Whether the full list is exhaustive; exact origins, numbers and individual duties; what distinguishes confirmed Ancients from figures only believed to be Ancients.

**Jenny Willows**, **Genny Green Teeth** and **Annie Lantern** are names for the same Ancient, not three separate individuals.
