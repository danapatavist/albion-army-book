# Court of Antlers

## Established lore
The **Court of Antlers** is **Elfred Sidesaddle's personal guard and circle of highborn companions** within the Blaewen Ridings. They are **adult noblemen, typically aged approximately eighteen to thirty**, and are properly trained as fighting men. They are not merely decorative courtiers. The circle has a distinct, fashionable courtly culture and is associated with Elfred's social life.

**Robin Antlers**, Elfred's husband, embodies the ideal of a traditional heroic prince: gallant, handsome and well mannered. He is distinct from **Robin Fair**, the outlaw and folk-king associated with Field and Fenn.

## Developing or unresolved
The Court's formal command, heraldry, specific military equipment, method of admission, political standing, and battlefield rules remain undefined. Its connection to Alfran's newer Earls has been suggested, **not confirmed**.
