# High Church and Folk Faith

## Established lore
Albion has a tension between the organised **High Church** and older, local or folk religious practices. Argant and other legendary figures pass into the island's religious imagination as saints, gods or revered figures. Ancient barrows, surviving pylons and sleeping rulers complicate the difference between legend and physical reality.

Reformed stone churches can use round tables, connecting to Albion's custom of using shield-backs for plans and discussion.
