# Orchestras of the Four Hosts

## Established lore
**All four Albion factions have orchestras.** The tradition is not exclusive to Alfran's Blaewen Ridings.

## Unresolved
Instrumentation, repertoire, ceremonial or battlefield roles, organisation, and differences between the four hosts are not yet confirmed. No specific music style should be assigned to any host without further creator input.
