# Personal Crests and Tattoos

Albion is a **respect-based society** in which personal reputation and one's word have considerable social weight. People **often wear a personal crest tattoo on the back of their hand**, a mark of individual identity rather than necessarily a formal family coat of arms.

Other people may add their **signatures or witness marks** to a person's skin, so the body acts like a collection of **receipts** for dealings and relationships. These can take the form of **long Ogham-like marks** along the body. An older or unusually long-lived person may accumulate **a line of such receipts running up the arm** from the hand: a visible record built over a lifetime.

The exact designs, conventions, meanings, and rules governing who may add a mark have not yet been established.
