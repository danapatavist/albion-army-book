# Personal Crests and Tattoos

Albion is a **respect-based society** in which personal reputation and one's word have considerable social weight. People **often wear a personal tattoo as their personal crest**, a mark of individual identity rather than necessarily a formal family coat of arms.

The exact designs, rules of inheritance, and uses of these tattoos have not yet been established.
