# Witches and Hags

## Established lore
In Albion, **hag** is another name for a **witch**, not a distinct species or confirmed higher magical rank. **Bog Hags** are simply witches of the marshes, not a separate magical species or order. Witches are distinct from the institutional **druids** and are generally cheaper tabletop magic choices.

An individual may also be an Ancient: **Jenny/Genny Willows**, also known as **Genny Green Teeth** and **Annie Lantern**, is one person and one of the Ancients.

## Everyday role and reputation

Witches are generally **cheaper to hire than druids** and often **more useful for a specific practical need**, such as **removing a wart**. Their magic tends to be particular rather than broad. They can also be **mischievous and difficult to deal with**. A witch is generally **paid if her magic works**—or may secure payment regardless through **how frightened the client is of her**. The balance between results and intimidation varies by witch. These qualities do not make their craft simply inferior to druidry.

## Forbidden witchcraft and dunking

**Penis-shrinking magic** is one of several **illegal spells** in Albion. A witch caught using forbidden spells can be **dunked** as punishment. The full list of prohibited spells, the authority overseeing punishment and how dunking is performed have not yet been established.

## Witchcraft and its vectors
Witches are **intuitive magic users**. Some seem born with a gift, while others develop or learn it; both occur. Their spells generally work through a **physical vector or focus**, such as a dolly, a knotted rope, eggs, or another ordinary object made significant by the witch's practice. These objects vary with the individual witch, rather than forming one standardised college discipline. **Jack's mother is a bean witch**; the exact use of beans in her practice is not yet established.


## Developing tabletop magic concept — not canon rules
Witches might use a small common **Petty Witchcraft** spell list, supplemented by one or two signature spells tied to their physical vector (bean, egg, dolly, knotted rope, etc.). This is a proposal only; spell names, effects, casting values and balance are unapproved. It should remain compatible in spirit with Warhammer Fantasy 6th-edition casting and dispelling rather than assume a new rules system.

## Unresolved
How instruction or inheritance works in particular communities; the precise range of their magic; whether a witch can cast without a vector; how the term *hag* varies socially or regionally.
