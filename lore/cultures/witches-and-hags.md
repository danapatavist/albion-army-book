# Witches and Hags

## Established lore
In Albion, **hag** is another name for a **witch**, not a distinct species or confirmed higher magical rank. Witches are distinct from the institutional **druids** and are generally cheaper tabletop magic choices.

An individual may also be an Ancient: **Jenny/Genny Willows**, also known as **Genny Green Teeth** and **Annie Lantern**, is one person and one of the Ancients.

## Witchcraft and its vectors
Witches are **intuitive magic users**. Some seem born with a gift, while others develop or learn it; both occur. Their spells generally work through a **physical vector or focus**, such as a dolly, a knotted rope, eggs, or another ordinary object made significant by the witch's practice. These objects vary with the individual witch, rather than forming one standardised college discipline.

## Unresolved
How instruction or inheritance works in particular communities; the precise range of their magic; whether a witch can cast without a vector; how the term *hag* varies socially or regionally.
