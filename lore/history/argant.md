# Argant and the Founding Tradition

## Established lore
**Argant** is a legendary founding king of Albion, credited with driving the Fimir towards the salt coasts. His deeds have become bound up in the religious traditions of the island: older heroes and rulers become saints, gods or revered ancestral figures.

**Sarfareth**, the old rememberer, betrayed Argant. Later traditions also associate Argant with the round shield that gives its name to the **Argant round shield**.

## Unresolved
Exact historical chronology, the nature of Sarfareth's betrayal, and the distinction between verifiable events and church/folk versions are still open.
