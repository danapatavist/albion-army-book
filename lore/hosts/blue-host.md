# Blue Host

## Established lore
Associated with **Alfran Longbritches**, an energetic moderniser, and his court. Warriors may be painted; units include javelin troops, war dogs, the Waiting Guard and javelin-armed ram riders. The allied giant **Tam Loggins** is associated with the Blue sphere. Alfran rides **Shaggy**, a long-necked lake creature that bears his throne.

Alfran's younger brother **Elfred Sidesaddle** belongs to this courtly world, as does **the Beast**, who acts as Elfred's natural guardian.

## Unresolved
Do not assume every Blue unit is under Alfran's direct personal command. Exact political borders, military hierarchy and titles are unconfirmed.
