# The Four Hosts

Albion is approached through four overlapping military and cultural hosts. They are not simply modern states or rigidly separated species.

## Blue
Alfran Longbritches' reforming sphere: painted fighters, javelins, war dogs, Waiting Guard and ram riders. [Blue Host](blue-host.md).

## Red
Maddoc the Flame's fiery sphere: fire breathers, ember druids, painted women slingers, Shrikers and chariots. [Red Host](red-host.md).

## Green
The Forest and Fenn, associated with Robin Fair, reed boats, fox masks, hags, woodsmen and bog magic. [Green Host](green-host.md).

## Grey
Older, uncannier forces: Wild Hunt, giants, kelpies, drowned warriors, Ancients, coast spirits and weather magic. [Grey Host](grey-host.md).

## Unresolved
Formal territorial borders, diplomatic arrangements and membership rules should not be invented.
