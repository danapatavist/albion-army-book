# Green Host — Forest and Fenn

## Established lore
The Green Host draws on the forests, marshes and folk traditions of Albion. Its recurring people include **Robin Fair**, **Big John**, outlaw woodsmen with paper fox masks, **Dykejumpers**, reed-boat fighters, short willow bows, bog imps and will-o'-wisps. Its witchcraft includes hags, fenn witches and the casters called **Root and Knots**.

Robin Fair is an outlaw/folk-king in prehistoric clothing with a short bronze blade; he refuses horses. **Jenny Willows** travels by smoky, wisp-haunted boat and has a notably long oar arm.

## Unresolved
The exact command structure and Robin Fair's formal authority are not settled.
