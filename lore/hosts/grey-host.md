# Grey Host

## Established lore
The Grey Host gathers Albion's older magical and uncanny powers rather than presenting a conventional modern army. Associated beings include **the Wild Hunt**, giants, kelpies, drowned **Waiting Guard**, coastal hags and shore kings, **Old Shannok**, the **Ancients**, and mist/weather phenomena.

Old Shannok is a blind wood wizard with white eyes and long nails on a moving throne of suspended stones joined by vines. The Wild Hunt is led by an undying Sutton Hoo-inspired king with a boat-chariot, retinue and legendary beasts.

## Unresolved
The precise chain of command and the relationship between the Grey Host and the other hosts are open.
