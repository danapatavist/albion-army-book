# Red Host

## Established lore
The Red Host centres on **Maddoc the Flame**, a female leader, and imagery of fire, painted warriors, fire breathers and ember druids. Its military themes include painted women slingers, boar chariots, Fire Maidens and the frightening female **Shrikers** with long polearms and flames. The **Red Queen** rides a bronze chariot drawn by she-lions. A **Bloodmaiden/Bloodflame** sorceress is associated with an ox- or boar-drawn cart.

## Unresolved
Maddoc's exact relation to the title Red Queen, the names and distinctions between Bloodmaiden/Bloodflame, and final army organisation need confirmation.
