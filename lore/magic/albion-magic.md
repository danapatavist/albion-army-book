# Albion Magic

## Working design direction

Albion will **probably have its own shared Lore of Magic**, using Warhammer-style **spell cards drawn from a deck** rather than unique complete spell lists for every named character.

Druids, witches and Ancients have different magical traditions and identities; **how they access or cast spells may differ**, but those mechanics are not yet established. In particular, do not assume Jenny Willows needs her own separate lore simply because she is an Ancient.

This is a **provisional rules direction**, not a confirmed spell list or casting system.

## Unresolved

Contents of the deck, casting rules, distinction between druids, witches and Ancients, any signature spells, and interaction with existing Warhammer lores.
