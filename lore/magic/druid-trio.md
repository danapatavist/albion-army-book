# Druid, Novice and Sword

## Established lore
**College-trained druids** normally operate in appointed trios: a **Druid**, a **Novice**, and a **Sword**, the Druid's armed guardian. The members are appointed through the druidic colleges, rather than necessarily having grown up together. **Solo druids are rarer**, but do exist. They may be self-taught, have left their druidic cult, or have been expelled. A solitary practice is therefore not a single school or background.

The **Novice** has limited magical skill. He is principally an assistant who supports the Druid's casting, rather than a second independent wizard. He is traditionally gelded. A Novice can eventually advance to become a Druid in his own right. The **Sword** is responsible for protecting the Druid and may place himself in harm's way for him. A Sword is **sworn to his Druid for life**; this is a lifelong personal oath, not a routine assignment.

Witches are a different tradition and are generally cheaper tabletop choices; they should not be treated as druids with different names.

## Developing gameplay ideas — NOT settled rules
- A Novice might provide an extra casting roll or equivalent support to the Druid.
- Operating alone, a Novice might know a very small selection of **petty spells**.
- The Sword may absorb wounds intended for the Druid, but the exact rules have not been decided.

## Unresolved
Specific spells, power levels, casting mechanics, allocation of wounds and what happens when members of the trio are separated.
