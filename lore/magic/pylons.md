# Pylons of the Old Ones

## Established lore
Ancient pylons hold back Chaos and are maintained, or at least watched over, by immortal beings. Many have weathered into the landscape and no longer look technological or even like recognisable pylons.

A pylon may now be mistaken for a **barrow, standing stone, ruined hill fort, witch-hut, stony cave, sheep fold or other old masonry**. Wolves frequently den around these locations and provide an ordinary, non-magical association with them.

Minor, damaged or badly aligned pylons can become weak points for **Beastman ingress**.
