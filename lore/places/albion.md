# Albion

Albion is an ancient island of barrows, marshes, forests, coasts, standing stones and buried works of the Old Ones. Its political and supernatural life is conventionally discussed through the Blue, Red, Green and Grey Hosts. Bronze Age and Britonnic material culture dominates the setting, even as reformers introduce foreign ideas.

## Established lore
- Many apparently ordinary structures are remnants of an ancient pylon network.
- Old folk faith and organised High Church traditions exist in tension.
- The island is guarded by the Ancients, although their capacities and motives vary.
- Argant's founding legend and his campaigns against the Fimir shape later religion.

## Unresolved
Exact geography, capital, boundaries between hosts, calendar and political constitution remain to be defined.
