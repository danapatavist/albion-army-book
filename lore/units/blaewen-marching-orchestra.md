# Blaewen Marching Orchestra

## Established lore

The Blaewen Ridings have a distinctive **marching orchestra** that accompanies their forces. It is one example of a broader Albion tradition: **all four hosts have orchestras**.

The Blaewen musicians dress in richly embroidered **blue and cream**, with cloaks, loose trousers and elaborate ceremonial clothing rather than medieval uniforms. The ensemble includes **large bronze horns, pipes, drums and bells**, a magnificent **dragon-headed instrument**, and an ornamented **bell-hung standard topped by a stag**.

**The musicians carry swords.** They can defend themselves if the enemy reaches them; they are not merely unarmed attendants.

## Developing tabletop concept

The orchestra may be a separate unit distinct from the ordinary individual musicians who accompany infantry regiments. Its precise morale effects, combat ability, classification and costs are not established. Do not assign game statistics or special rules without approval.
