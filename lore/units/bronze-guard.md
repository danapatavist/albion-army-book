# Bronze Guard
The **Bronze Guard** is a full elite regiment of the Red Host commanded by **Bredda**, Maddoc the Flame's large, strong warrior sister. Their unusual armour resembles **overlapping pub brasses**, decorative hammered brass plates with rustic imagery. This visual is important: do not turn them into generic medieval plate-armoured knights. Bredda is also a playable character in her own right. Rules and exact equipment remain undecided.
