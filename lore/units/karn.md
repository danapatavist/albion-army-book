# Karn

## Established lore
**Karn** is Alfran Longbritches' enormous, extravagantly built siege **battering ram** of the Blaewen Ridings. It has an immense **A-frame** timber superstructure, **enormous wheels**, and a monumental bronze **ram's head** with dramatically curling horns. The head is used to break fortifications. It is a functional siege machine, but deliberately far grander and larger than necessary: an occasional expression of Alfran's vanity rather than his usual practical reforming temperament.

Despite its enormous scale, its name is simply **Karn**, spoken as familiarly as the name of a farm dog. **“All for Karn!”** is a proposed battle cry associated with its appearance.

Its style remains **Anglo-Celtic / Bronze Age Albion**, not medieval or Arthurian. The ram's construction and ornamentation should fit the exaggerated, chunky silhouette of classic fantasy wargame miniatures. The existing A-frame, great-wheeled miniature concept is the working visual direction; no private art is deposited in the repository.

## Developing tabletop concept
Karn is a distinct Blaewen siege war machine, not the smaller two-ram missile chariot or Alfran's ceremonial wagon. Crew numbers, exact suspension of the striking beam, movement, battlefield rules and point cost are undetermined.
