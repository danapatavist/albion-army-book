# Long Long Bow Champions

## Host availability
**Long Long Bow Champions and Woodchopper Champions are shared elite traditions of the Blue and Green Hosts.** Red and Grey do not normally field these champion units. This restriction applies to the champion formations, not necessarily to ordinary archers or their common equipment.

## Established lore
Albion's ordinary archers are a standard troop type, with **bow**, **sling and round shield**, or **javelin and round shield** as proposed equipment paths. Beyond these ordinary bowmen stand the **Long Long Bow Champions**, an elite rank of celebrated archers. This is a **two-tier** distinction, not three tiers: there is no separate intermediate "Bow Champion" troop type.

Champions are associated with contests of marksmanship, rather like the great archery competitions in Robin Hood tales. They can be older or former tournament champions, whose skill and reputations outlast the contests themselves. A line of champions is an elite missile formation.

**Their bows are personal, not made to one standard length.** One champion might commission a monstrously long, costly bow with an assistant to help manage it; another has a perfectly ordinary-sized bow and extraordinary skill. Robin Fair is the latter sort of archer: his own bow is not enormous, he is simply remarkably good. The extravagant two-person bow is an example, **not a required unit configuration**.

**Every Long Long Bow Champion has his own bird.** The bird is personally trained and helps its champion locate targets through Albion's smog and mist, which Albion armies may bring with them. Its calls, circling, and movements signal where enemies are even when the archer cannot see them directly. Birds are intended to be **difficult to kill** in play, though not necessarily invulnerable.

Other Albion archers may be aided by trained birds, animal Binders or warg spotters. A genuine Bird Binder can perceive through an animal's senses, unlike an ordinary trained bird, which communicates through learned signals. Do not assume every champion is a magical Binder.

## Proposed tabletop rule — Eyes in the Mist
Albion bowmen may ignore mist- or fog-related penalties to shooting **when a friendly spotter has located the target**. For Long Long Bow Champions, their own trained birds provide this spotting capacity. The precise mechanics, range, how an opponent can target or drive off the bird, and which kinds of concealment this bypasses are **not yet decided**. Do not treat this as a universal ability to ignore all cover or line-of-sight rules.

## Related sporting champions
**Woodchopper Champions** are another elite formation: dangerous axe-men whose prestige comes from chopping competitions and practical mastery, not necessarily from noble birth or military service. Sporting championships can confer genuine authority and celebrity in Albion.

## Unresolved
Exact points, profiles, rule wording, any exceptional cross-host recruitment, bird survivability and limits remain open for playtesting.
