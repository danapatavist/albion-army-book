# Blaewen Plaid Men

## Established lore
The **Plaid Men** are the Blaewen Ridings' **paid professional infantry**. They remain fundamentally the same kind of soldiers as Albion's ordinary Painted Warriors, but are better provided for under Alfran's organised military system.

Their distinctive appearance includes **tartan trousers worn beneath short kilts**, **bronze helmets**, **somewhat more armour**, **moustaches**, and ordinary Albion **round shields**. Their weapons remain within the familiar Albion military tradition; they are not medieval plate-armoured infantry.

Their name deliberately suggests both **plaid** cloth and **paid** soldiers.

## Unresolved
Exact recruitment, whether moustaches are formally required, equipment variations, battlefield stats, command structure and troop costs are undecided.
