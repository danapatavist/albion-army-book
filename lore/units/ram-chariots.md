# Ram Chariots

## Established lore
**Ram-drawn chariots** are part of the Blaewen Ridings' military and aristocratic imagery. A lord might ride an **Oc'nesse**, or use a **ram chariot**. These chariots are distinct from **javelin-armed ram riders**, which are also associated with Blue-aligned forces.

Alfran's treasure convoy and his transportation of the entire Sword in the Stone likewise include ram-drawn wagon imagery. Do not assume all ram-drawn wagons are battlefield chariots.

## Unresolved
Crew, weapons, exact ram breeds, battle role, troop category, and game statistics remain undecided.

## Battlefield chariot design
A working Blaewen war chariot is drawn by **two large rams**, with a **two-person crew**: a driver and a missile-armed warrior. The warrior can carry **a bow, sling or javelins** as model/weapon options. The low chariot body is more **sled-like** than a boat, with only a restrained Anglo-Celtic hint of a dragon-boat in its timbers, and its **rear is open** so warriors can jump down. A deliberately exaggerated **dragon-headed draught pole** forms the dragon's long neck, protruding absurdly far forward. Avoid a full Viking longship on wheels; the wider Albion aesthetic is Anglo-Celtic, not conventionally Viking. The large courtly wagons and ceremonial chariots remain distinct from this practical battlefield model.
