# Ram Chariots

## Established lore
**Ram-drawn chariots** are part of the Blaewen Ridings' military and aristocratic imagery. A lord might ride an **Oc'nesse**, or use a **ram chariot**. These chariots are distinct from **javelin-armed ram riders**, which are also associated with Blue-aligned forces.

Alfran's treasure convoy and his transportation of the entire Sword in the Stone likewise include ram-drawn wagon imagery. Do not assume all ram-drawn wagons are battlefield chariots.

## Unresolved
Crew, weapons, exact ram breeds, battle role, troop category, and game statistics remain undecided.
