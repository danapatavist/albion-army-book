# The Seventeen Standards

## Established lore
The **Seventeen Standards — Winter Household Regiment** is the **Empress's personal elite guard**, one formidable household regiment rather than seventeen separate units. It is distinct from Alfran's ordinary soldiers.

The Empress claims seventeen northern dukedoms through her heraldry. Her regiment displays **a profusion of great standards, flags, pennants and streamers**, with an extravagant mixture of **Celtic/northern** devices: animals, spirals, geometric marks and bold patchwork colours. Its troops wear northern furs, helmets and round shields. This is emphatically **not Bretonnian medieval knightly heraldry**.

The abundant heraldry may be amusing, but the regiment consists of genuinely capable fighters.

## Unresolved
Exact troop size, arms distribution, composition, rules and the identity of its northern recruits are not yet settled.
