# Widow Carts

## Established concept
The Red Host's **Widow Carts** are small, crude **wheelbarrow-like conveyances**, not grand chariots. A widow pushes her **dead husband** into battle in a rough wooden barrow. The corpse is carried on its last journey into the fighting, and the cart is **set burning and sent toward the enemy**.

Some Widow Carts may also carry **a barrel of oil**, adding the prospect of an explosive fiery end. Keep this as a possible variation, not mandatory equipment.

Their impact is intensely personal: **one widow, one dead husband, one burning barrow**. They express grief, anger and a funerary send-off through the Red Host's use of fire. They are not undead warriors or magically animated corpses.

## Visual direction
- Rough wooden wheelbarrow, plain and practical rather than a grand war chariot.
- The husband's body is plainly carried inside.
- A woman actively pushes or launches the barrow.
- Combustible materials; some examples may have a barrel of oil.
- Red Host folk character, rough utilitarian construction; avoid polished or medieval styling.

## Unresolved
Exact ignition and delivery procedure, the extent of oil use, ritual customs, and the fate of the widow are not yet fixed. **Do not assign tabletop rules or unit categories at this stage.**
