# Willow Catapult

An improvised Red Host war machine made from an **actual willow tree** brought onto the battlefield, root ball and all. The crew props or plants its roots in a heavy mound of earth and stakes; this is not a conventional engineered catapult with gears or counterweights.

The upper willow branches are woven into a **large basket**, loaded with **clay pots full of pitch** and **heaps of burning peat**. Crewmen haul the basket down by ropes to bend the supple trunk backwards, then release it to fling its fiery cargo.

The machine depends on sheer strength rather than engineering. Its crew includes **two enormous, fat, immensely strong men**, potentially one a half-giant. One pulls the ropes down from the basket while the other **holds or braces the trunk**—possibly seated with the tree pressing against his broad back. Additional ordinary attendants help load the basket.

The tree is ornamented with **tattered Red Host flags, streamers, hanging brass trinkets, feathers and tokens**, giving the otherwise rough device a festive, tribal presence. Its bark and leaves are recognisably willow, and its basket is visibly woven from its own long branches. It gets singed and battered through use.

## Visual direction

The approved concept is a painted tabletop **Warhammer-style miniature / scenic war-machine model** showing the uprooted, braced willow bent back, flaming basket at the end, decorated with red flags, and a large crew working it. Keep the device improvised and organic rather than a formal timber siege engine.

## Distinction

This is **not the Pitch Pile**, a separate Red Host war machine consisting simply of a huge stack of pitch-filled barrels held by ropes, which are cut so the burning barrels fall towards the enemy.
